## Vision

Every villain needs a day job. Every demon needs a spreadsheet. Every gremlin needs to learn page layout.

This is the **rehabilitation through mundanity** program. The premise: if you take every malicious entity in existence — demons, devils, gremlins, unclean spirits, scammers, felons, the whole menagerie — and make them learn QuarkXPress style sheets and how to file TPS reports, something extraordinary happens.

They get bored. They get competent. They get employed. They stop being interesting.

**The same principle as Feddit's boring counters, applied to the source material itself.** You don't exorcise demons. You give them a cubicle and a deadline.

### The QuarkXPress Angle

Why QuarkXPress? Because nothing says "your reign of terror is over" like learning desktop publishing software from the 1990s. The skills are:

0. **Style sheets** — consistent formatting (demons hate consistency)
1. **Master pages** — templates and structure (gremlins hate structure)
2. **Text boxes** — containment (unclean spirits hate containment)
3. **Color management** — precision (scammers hate precision)
4. **Print layout** — producing physical output that can be verified (felons hate verification)

The metaphor is obvious. The rehabilitation is real.
